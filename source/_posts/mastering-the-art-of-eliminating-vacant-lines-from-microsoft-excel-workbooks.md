##  Automatically Delete All Empty Rows in Excel

 Excel offers an automatic method that finds and [deletes all the blank rows](https://fox-glue.techidaily.com/2024-approved-the-ultimate-guide-to-top-online-tools-for-perfecting-your-video-subtitles/) in your worksheet. This way, you don't have to manually find any empty rows and select them to delete---something that's too time-consuming if you have a large dataset.

 To use this method, first, launch your spreadsheet with Microsoft Excel. Then, select the dataset where you want to find and remove [empty rows](https://extra-information.techidaily.com/new-brand-alliances-elevating-youtube-content/).

![Highlight the dataset.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/1-select-dataset-excel.png) 

 While your dataset is highlighted, in Excel's ribbon at the top, select the "Home" tab.

![Access the "Home" tab.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/2-excel-home-tab.png) 

 On the "Home" tab, in the "Editing" section, choose Find & Select > Go To Special.

![Select Find & Select > Go To Special.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/3-excel-go-to-special.png) 

 A "Go To Special" window will open. Here, enable the "Blanks" option, then choose "OK" at the bottom.

![Enable "Blanks" and click "OK."](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/4-excel-go-to-blank-rows.png) 

 All the blank rows in your selected dataset are now highlighted.

![All blank rows highlighted in the dataset.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/5-highlighted-blank-rows-excel.png)

##  Manually Delete Blank Rows in Excel

 If you don't have a large dataset, and you prefer manually [cleaning the empty rows](https://facebook-video-recording.techidaily.com/how-to-maximize-revenue-the-secret-of-fb-animation-campaigns/), you have the option to do that.

 In your Excel worksheet, to the extreme left of the blank row you want to delete, click the [row number](https://howto.techidaily.com/play-store-not-working-on-motorola-razr-40-8-solutions-inside-drfone-by-drfone-fix-android-problems-fix-android-problems/). This selects that entire row.

![Click the row number.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/8-select-row-number.png) 

 To [add more rows to your selection](https://win-howtos.techidaily.com/how-to-fix-timeout-issue-during-download-progress/), press and hold down Ctrl (Windows) or Command (Mac) on your keyboard and click your row numbers.

![Select additional rows.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/9-select-multiple-rows-excel.png) 

 When your rows are highlighted, [right-click](https://desktop-recording.techidaily.com/updated-the-art-of-recording-fun-6-techniques-to-document-minecraft-for-2024/) any one blank row's number and choose "Delete" in the menu.
